Lawbenpina Ventures is an Accra-based business founded in 2017.

Our story

Lawbenpina Ventures started as a consulting and investment venture and has grown into an import service. If there is something you want to bring in from China, Turkey, or elsewhere abroad, we source it, arrange the logistics, and get it to you.

The business is owned and run by Addo Benjamin Armah, and it is built on responsiveness and follow-through on every order.
